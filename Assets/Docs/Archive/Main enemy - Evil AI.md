# Goal:
To make the player play tactically, and force them to make tough decisions and explore different engagement options.
# Systems:
## Power struggle:
The AI could have limited power and such, fighting back, or making it waste resources could weaken them.
## Player tracking:
The AI doesn't always know the player's position, it needs to use different tools to observe them and track their position. Those include:
- Cameras, which can be destroyed
- Patrol Drones
- Motion Sensors
- Electric Doors
- Noise Sensors
## Engaging the player:
The AI's goal to have fun with the player, it doesn't want to immediately kill it. As such, it's actions should force the player to react, instead of being strictly punishing.
### Ideas
#### Turrets
They shoot, when they see the player.
##### Player Reactions:
- They can shoot and destroy them
- They can hide and wait for them to disapear
- They can find an alternative route

Game Design Concept: Stealth Escape Facility
- Core Gameplay: Strategic escape from a facility with AI-controlled obstacles
- Key Design Elements:
  * Static obstacles that players must strategically navigate
  * AI that dynamically responds to player actions
  * Multiple approach paths through rooms
  * Tools for player defense (EMP, shields, explosives)

Unique AI Mechanics:
- AI hones in on player position when detected
- Gradually increases obstacles and traps
- Can lock doors, deploy turrets, or release gas
- Designed to create tension without direct chase mechanics

Technical Design Considerations:
- Most doors will be open initially
- Potential for robotic enemies with predictable movement patterns
- Laser traps as potential "mines"
- Inspiration drawn from Watch dogs Legion design (multiple approach paths)

Player Interaction Strategies:
- Player can potentially:
  * Use shields to absorb turret shots
  * Deploy EMPs to disable systems
  * Use explosives to open doors
  * Potentially scare or distract AI enemies

Design Philosophy:
- Avoid time pressure
- Encourage tactical thinking
- Create a unique gameplay experience
- Allow multiple strategies for progression

Potential Gameplay Mechanics:
- AI becomes more aggressive as player is detected
- Gradual increase in facility's defensive complexity
- Tools that allow creative problem-solving
- Stealth as a primary gameplay element
